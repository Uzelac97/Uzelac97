**AI automation.** Workflows that take repetitive work off small businesses: AI document and photo analysis, quote generation, email handling and approvals, built with n8n and the Claude API.

### ⭐ Featured projects

**[Bookilo](https://bookilo-saas.vercel.app/)**: multi-tenant booking SaaS for barbershops and other appointment-based businesses.
- Flat-fee pricing, no commission: a direct alternative to Fresha and Treatwell
- Real-time availability, buffer time enforced at the database level with a Postgres exclusion constraint, transactional email via Resend
- Built solo end to end: architecture, backend, frontend, deployment, ops

**[Summit Roofing – AI Quote Generator](https://github.com/Uzelac97/summit-roofing-ai-quote-generator)**: turns a customer's roof photo and description into a priced PDF quote.
- Claude analyzes the photo and description; prices come only from the business's own price list
- Owner reviews every quote, can correct quantities, and the revised PDF is sent automatically
- Confidence and urgency flags, error alerts and a quote log in Google Sheets
