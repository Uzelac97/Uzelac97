### 🔭 What I'm building

**[Bookilo](https://bookilo-saas.vercel.app/)** — a multi-tenant booking SaaS for barbershops, expanding to other appointment-based service businesses.

- Flat-fee pricing, no commission — a direct alternative to Fresha and Treatwell
- Live in production: real-time availability, buffer-time enforced at the database level with a Postgres exclusion constraint, transactional email via Resend
- Built solo end to end — architecture, backend, frontend, deployment, ops
